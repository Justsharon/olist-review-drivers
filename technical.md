# Olist Review Drivers

**What factors predict 1-star reviews on Olist?**
An end-to-end analysis identifying the strongest drivers of customer dissatisfaction in Brazilian e-commerce.

## Status
In progress — Sprint 1 (May 3 – May 17, 2026)

## Business Question
Which factors among delivery performance, product attributes, seller reliability, and order characteristics have the strongest measurable association with 1-star reviews?

## Why It Matters
1-star reviews are leading indicators of customer churn and damage conversion for future buyers. This analysis produces a prioritized list of intervention targets — fix the top drivers, measurably reduce 1-star rates.

## Dataset
[Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) — ~100k orders, 9 CSVs covering orders, customers, sellers, products, reviews, and payments.
